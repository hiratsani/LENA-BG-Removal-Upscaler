More on GitHub
Is LENA really free?
Yes. Background removal and upscaling are free to use, with no account, no credits, no ads, and no subscription. There are no usage limits.
Does it work without an internet connection?
After installation, Basic removal, Hair & Fur, and SPAN upscaling work offline—their models are included. Better and Best are optional downloads. Once downloaded, they can also be reused offline. Images are processed locally.
What do I need to run it?
A 64-bit PC running Windows 10 or Windows 11, and enough storage for the app and its AI models. A GPU with DirectX 12 support is recommended. Processing time depends on your hardware, image size, and selected quality.
Which formats can I save?
Export as PNG to preserve transparency, or JPG for a solid image. You can preview a removed background on transparent, white, or black before saving.
Why might Windows show a publisher warning?
LENA is currently distributed without a code-signing certificate, so Windows SmartScreen may show an “Unknown Publisher” warning. The official download is available in the project's GitHub release.
